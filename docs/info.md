## How it works

A CMOS inverter (the AD104 Lab 03 cell) between ua[0] (input A) and ua[1] (output Y), powered from VDPWR and VGND.

## How to test

Drive ua[0] between 0 and 1.8 V and watch ua[1] go the other way.
